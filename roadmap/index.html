ILoveNotch for Windows

A separate app with the same themes, as an island at the top of each monitor.

Apple’s Dynamic Island

The new OLED MacBook Pro has a real Dynamic Island instead of a notch, and macOS 27.3 brings live activities to every Apple silicon Mac’s menu bar. ILoveNotch will sit next to Apple’s island rather than on top of it, can send its timer, teleprompter and agent alerts to Apple’s activities, and gets a Dynamic Island theme of its own.

On Windows, later

A separate app, since the Mac one is native SwiftUI: an island at the top of each monitor, with media, the shelf, clipboard, timers, AI usage and the same theme files. Windows lets an app read notifications with your permission, so that one comes too.

Not building

What we won’t build, and why.

Some features other apps have would cost privacy, App Store safety, or the 0.0% CPU at rest. These are the ones we decided against.

Copying macOS notifications

No public API lets an app read other apps' notifications. The workarounds scrape Notification Center through Accessibility, hide the system's own banners, and break with macOS updates.

Instead: Show in Notch, and macOS 27's Shortcuts trigger for notifications.

Seen in Boring Notch, Droppy, Notchy, vorssaint-utils

Brightness HUD and lock-screen widgets

Both need Apple's private frameworks, which aren't allowed on the App Store and can break with any update.

Instead: The volume display, which uses public APIs.

Seen in NotchNook Pro, Droppy, Notchy, Alcove

WhatsApp, Messages and Discord in the notch

They need unofficial access to someone else's service, against its terms, and can put your account at risk.

Seen in NotchNook Pro, Droppy, Notchy, Notchify

Usage tracking and analytics

No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. The network is used only for updates, the AI providers you turn on, and sharing on your own Wi-Fi.

License servers and trials

The direct download is free and MIT licensed. The App Store edition is a one-time $1.99.

Cloud links and accounts

Everything stays on your Mac, or on your local network when you share with a phone.

Seen in Droppy

Other apps' live activities

Apple offers no way to read another app's activities. ILoveNotch will publish its own to macOS 27.3 instead.

Seen in DynamicLake

Always-on camera features

The camera is on only while the Mirror tab is open. Face unlock would need it all the time.

Seen in Notchy

Window managers, uninstallers, fan control

Great tools, but not a notch's job. Keeping the app small is how it stays at 0.0% CPU.

Seen in vorssaint-utils, Notchy, Droppy

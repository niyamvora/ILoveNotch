Live activities

It speaks up only when it matters.

Closed, the notch hides behind the camera until it has something to tell you. One-shot alerts drop in as a row under the camera; ongoing ones, like a call or a timer, sit as a tab beside it, icon and text together.

  • Volume and charging
  • Calls about to start
  • Agents waiting for you
  • AI limits at 80% and 95%
  • Accessory batteries
  • Your own, from Shortcuts

Built for developers

Your AI limits and agents, under the camera.

AI Usage shows how much of each coding plan you've used, with resets and pace. Agents taps you on the shoulder when Claude Code or Codex is waiting for an approval, and one click takes you back to its terminal.

ClaudeCodexCursorCopilotAntigravityDevinGrokOllamaOpenCodeOpenRouterZ.ai

Every provider is off until you add it, and each sign-in goes only to its own service. Agents never read your prompts or code. Both are in the direct download, since the App Store’s sandbox doesn’t allow them.

Private by design

It stays on your Mac.

Permissions are asked for only when you turn on the feature that needs them. The full details are in the privacy policy.

No accounts, no analytics

No telemetry and no device fingerprinting. Ever.

Three network uses

Update checks, the AI providers you add, and sharing on your own Wi-Fi.

Camera only in Mirror

Off until you turn it on, and on only while that tab is open.

Clipboard is opt-in

Off until you turn it on, and it skips passwords.
